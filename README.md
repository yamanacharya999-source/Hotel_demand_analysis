Excited to share a project I recently built: a **Hotel Booking Demand Analysis dashboard** comparing City Hotel and Resort Hotel performance to uncover what's really driving cancellations, guest loyalty, and revenue health.

𝗧𝗵𝗲 𝗢𝗯𝗷𝗲𝗰𝘁𝗶𝘃𝗲
To move beyond raw numbers and understand the "why" behind booking behavior — cancellations, repeat guests, and room demand — across two very different hotel segments.

𝗪𝗵𝗮𝘁 𝗜 𝗕𝘂𝗶𝗹𝘁
Using Power BI, I designed an interactive dashboard tracking:
• Cancellation count, repeat guests, and week-night stays
• Cancellation rate by arrival month and market segment
• Weeknight vs. weekend stay patterns
• Repeat guest distribution by country
• Room demand stability across segments

𝗞𝗲𝘆 𝗙𝗶𝗻𝗱𝗶𝗻𝗴𝘀
• City Hotel saw 3x higher cancellations than Resort Hotel (33K vs. 11K), despite a similar repeat-guest base
• Online Travel Agents drove the majority of cancellations at both properties (46–63%)
• City Hotel's room demand leaned heavily on a single room type (79%), while Resort Hotel stayed more balanced (62/38)
• Portugal emerged as the single largest source of repeat guests across both hotels

𝗣𝗿𝗼𝗯𝗹𝗲𝗺𝘀 𝘁𝗼 𝗦𝗼𝗹𝘃𝗲
High cancellation exposure, heavy OTA dependency, weak guest retention, and unbalanced room-type demand were the core issues surfaced by the data.

𝗢𝗽𝗽𝗼𝗿𝘁𝘂𝗻𝗶𝘁𝗶𝗲𝘀 𝗜𝗱𝗲𝗻𝘁𝗶𝗳𝗶𝗲𝗱
From doubling down on high-value markets like Portugal, to shifting bookings toward direct channels, to building a simple cancellation-risk flag — small, targeted moves that could meaningfully improve both revenue predictability and guest retention.

𝗧𝗵𝗲 𝗧𝗮𝗸𝗲𝗮𝘄𝗮𝘆
This project reinforced how much value sits inside operational data once it's visualized well — the same numbers that look like noise in a spreadsheet become a clear roadmap once you ask the right questions of them.
